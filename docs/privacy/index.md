# Privacy & GDPR for clubs

Your club is the **data controller** for your members' personal data. Whoever hosts RestAssured
for you is a **processor**. Also available in [Dutch](/nl/privacy/).

::: info Coming with the first release
The templates below will be published under CC0, so you can copy and adapt them freely.
:::
